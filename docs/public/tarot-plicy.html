隐私政策

生效日期 2026 年 10 月 29 日 · 适用版本 1.0.0 起

一句话：塔罗不收集、不上传、不共享你的任何个人信息。你抽了什么牌、写了什么问题，只留在这台设备上，我们看不到。

01适用范围

本政策适用于「塔罗」（Tarot）Android 应用，包名 run.oldbird.tarot，由 Oldbird 提供。

应用无需注册、无需登录，也没有账号体系。我们没有保存你数据的服务器，因此不存在「我们这边的数据库」这一说。

02应用在本机保存什么

以下内容保存在应用的私有目录（Hive 数据库），只有本应用能读取，其他应用和我们都无法访问：

  • 牌记：抽牌时间、牌阵类型、抽到的牌与正逆位、心境牌
  • 你输入的问句：如果在抽牌前写了问题，它会与这条牌记一起保存在本机
  • 偏好设置：外观（深色 / 浅色）、语言、牌面风格、抽牌方式
  • 评价提示节奏：启动次数、上次提示的时间

这些数据不会离开设备。我们不读取、不备份、不上传，也没有任何渠道可以读取它们。

03我们不收集什么

不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录；不收集精确或粗略位置；不访问相册、麦克风、摄像头；不读取设备标识符（IMEI、Android ID、广告 ID）；不收集应用使用统计与崩溃日志。

应用内没有广告 SDK、没有统计分析 SDK、没有崩溃上报 SDK。牌面图像与解读文案随安装包一起分发，不从网络加载。

04申请的权限

应用只申请一项权限：INTERNET（网络访问）。用途见下一节。应用不申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录、存储等任何权限。

05网络访问用在哪

只有一处：设置 → 检查更新。点击后，应用会向 play.google.com 查询本应用在商店中的最新版本号，用来告诉你是否有更新。

  • 请求只包含应用包名，不包含任何与你有关的信息。
  • 与任何网络请求一样，Google 的服务器会看到你的 IP 地址；该请求由 Google 按其自身隐私政策处理，我们既不读取也无法获取。
  • 如果你不点「检查更新」，应用不会主动发起任何网络请求。

06第三方组件

  • Google Play 应用内评价：由系统弹窗完成，评分与评论直接提交给 Google Play，不经过我们。
  • 打开商店页面：通过系统浏览器或 Play 商店打开，其行为受对应应用自身的隐私政策约束。

除以上两项由系统提供的交互外，应用不与其他第三方共享任何信息。

07儿童隐私

应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。由于应用本身不收集任何个人信息，这一承诺在技术上也同样成立。

08你的控制权

  • 删除单条牌记：在牌记页左滑删除。
  • 删除全部数据：卸载应用即清空全部本机数据。我们这边没有副本可删。
  • 因为我们从未持有你的数据，所以无法导出或代你删除；这既是限制，也是隐私上的保证。

09政策变更

如果未来版本引入需要联网或收集数据的功能（例如云同步、统计分析），本政策会在该版本发布前更新，并在应用内或商店页面提示。生效日期会同步修改。

01Scope

This policy covers the Tarot Android app, package name run.oldbird.tarot, provided by Oldbird.

The app has no sign-up, no login, and no account system. We run no server that stores your data, so there is no database on our side to speak of.

02What the app stores on your device

The following is kept in the app's private storage (a Hive database), readable only by this app:

  • Reading history: when you drew, which spread, which cards and whether reversed, the mood card
  • Questions you type: if you wrote a question before drawing, it is saved locally alongside that reading
  • Preferences: appearance (dark / light), language, card art style, draw method
  • Review prompt pacing: launch count and the time of the last prompt

None of this leaves your device. We do not read it, back it up, or upload it, and we have no channel through which to access it.

03What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts; not your precise or approximate location; we do not access your photos, microphone, or camera; we do not read device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID); we do not collect usage analytics or crash logs.

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. Card artwork and interpretation text ship inside the app package and are never fetched from a network.

04Permissions

The app requests a single permission: INTERNET. Its only use is described below. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

05The one place the network is used

Only in Settings → Check for updates. When you tap it, the app queries play.google.com for the latest version number of this app on the store, so it can tell you whether an update exists.

  • The request carries the package name only — nothing about you.
  • As with any network request, Google's servers see your IP address. That request is handled under Google's own privacy policy; we neither read it nor have access to it.
  • If you never tap "Check for updates", the app makes no network requests at all.

06Third-party components

  • Google Play In-App Review: handled by a system dialog; your rating and review go straight to Google Play, never through us.
  • Opening the store page: handled by your browser or the Play Store app, and subject to that app's own privacy policy.

Apart from these two system-provided interactions, the app shares nothing with any third party.

07Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, this holds by construction.

08Your control

  • Delete a single reading: swipe left on it in the history screen.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app wipes all local data. We hold no copy to delete.
  • Because we never held your data, we cannot export it or delete it on your behalf — a limitation, and also the point.

09Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that require the network or collect data (cloud sync, analytics), this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a notice in the app or on the store listing. The effective date will change accordingly.
